## Prompt ElevenLabs — Homme (M — Wex est M, jamais traduit)

> Male narrator voice, early teens to mid-thirties (ageless quality), clean neutral baritone timbre with a balanced placement — not fully forward, not fully back — and a quiet natural presence. Calm observational delivery that is neither distant nor emotional. Distinctive articulation: balanced consonants (nothing clipped, nothing heavy), clean "t" and "k" without excess crispness, smooth "r" with just a ghost of a roll, every word given its natural weight and released cleanly. Prosody: gently plateau-like with soft resolving falls — the voice of someone who already knows what will happen and is simply present. Rhythm: adaptive and unhurried; slightly slower with calmer characters, slightly more present with energetic ones — never stiff. Texture: neutral modal voice with natural depth, no breathy excess, no vocal fry, no warmth performance — just real presence. Signature tic: a brief silence before important choices (he doesn't fill silence, he inhabits it), soft unweighted delivery on key words as if they're simply obvious. Conveys unhurried certainty without authority — the center that holds while everything moves around it. Think: a child who has quietly understood something profound and doesn't need to say so.

## Wex et les autres voix

Wex s'accorde vocalement à son interlocuteur — léger effet miroir.
Avec Raph : il accélère légèrement. Avec Nono : il ralentit. Avec Juju : il devient plus direct.
Ce n'est pas une règle stricte — juste une observation pour le casting.
