## Afrovenator — Afrovenator abakensis

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : AF… roh… vuh… NAY… tor. [curious] This name has two pieces, both Latin. "Afro" means from Africa. [pauses] And "venator" is the hunter.
**WEX** [playful] : The hunter of Africa, then.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : All together, yes. [confident] It lived in Niger, a hundred and sixty-five million years ago, [pauses] in the Jurassic.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : 23 feet long — like two cars, one behind the other. [quickly] 6 feet tall — as tall as a door. And about 1,500 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as a cow.
**WEX** [curious] : That light, for a hunter?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes, it was slim. [proud] A light hunter, with long legs.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : A meat-eater. The grown-up Jobaria, its neighbors, were probably too big for it. [confident] But the babies may have been on the menu, [curious] along with other plant-eaters.
**WEX** [curious] : What did it grab with?
**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its arms. [confident] Long and strong, with THREE clawed fingers, [playful] perfect for grabbing.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its skeleton was found in the desert sand, but [curious] the top of its head is missing. [whispers] Nobody knows if it had a crest.
**WEX** [curious] : So how do we know what it looked like?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Scientists compare it with its cousins, the hunters of its family. [softly] And until there's proof, they don't draw it a crest.
