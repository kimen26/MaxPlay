## Amargasaurus — Amargasaurus cazaui

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Uh… MAR… guh… SOR… us. [curious] This name comes from a place! "La Amarga," in Argentina, where its bones were found.
**WEX** [curious] : The lizard from La Amarga.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Right. It lived in Argentina, a hundred twenty-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it have anything special about it?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Two rows of really big spikes, [amazed] running down its neck and its back.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Thirty-three feet long — [amazed] as long as a truck. Standing up, eight feet tall — [curious] as tall as a soccer goal. And three thousand kilos — [proud] as heavy as a hippo.
**WEX** [curious] : And how big were the spikes?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Up to two feet tall — [amazed] right in the MIDDLE of its neck. The tallest of any known sauropod.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, in a small group. [calm] It grazed on ground plants and bushes.
**WEX** [curious] : But what were the spikes actually for?
**NARRATEUR H** [hesitant] : [pauses] Scientists still aren't sure. [curious] Maybe for defense, swinging its neck. Or maybe to stand out to other Amargasaurus.
**WEX** [curious] : And if nobody knows, how do you pick?
**NARRATEUR H** [gently] : You don't pick. [calm] You share both ideas, and keep looking for the real answer.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Some scientists even think its spikes carried skin stretched between them — [pauses] like a strange sail on its back.
**WEX** [gasps] : A sail, on a dinosaur.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Maybe. [amazed] A living hedge of spikes, or a sail — the mystery's still wide open.
