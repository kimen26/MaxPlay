## Ankylosaurus — Ankylosaurus magniventris

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : ang… KY… loh… SOR… us. [curious] In Greek, "ankylo" means fused, all stiff.
**WEX** [curious] : The stiff lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Yep — its back was fused together like tough armor. [calm] It lived in North America, sixty-six million years ago.
**WEX** [gasps] : Sixty-six million? That's T. rex's time, right?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Exactly, [pauses] and Triceratops too. [amazed] All three lived in the same land, at the same time.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Twenty-five feet long — [amazed] as long as two cars, one behind the other. Standing up, six feet tall — [curious] as tall as Dad standing up. And six thousand kilos — [proud] as heavy as THREE rhinos. [calm] It wasn't fast at all: only about 4 miles an hour, like Dad walking fast.
**WEX** [amazed] : Not fast at all, then.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Nope. [serious] It didn't need to be — its armor did the job.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, mostly a loner. [calm] Its back was covered in bony plates, and its tail ended in a big club.
**WEX** [nervous] : Did T. rex dare attack it?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : It could try. [serious] But it was very risky: Ankylosaurus's weak spots — belly, eyes, legs — were all well hidden. And one swing of that club tail, [pauses] could break its leg.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Up top, it was a real fortress. [serious] But its belly had no armor at all.
**WEX** [hesitant] : So how did it protect itself?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : It stayed hugging the ground, low on its legs. [confident] Almost impossible to flip over.
