## Apatosaurus (Brontosaurus) — Apatosaurus louisae

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Uh… PAT… oh… SOR… us. [curious] In Greek, "apato" means trickery, an illusion.
**WEX** [curious] : The tricky lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Yep — [amazed] its bones fooled scientists for a really long time, [calm] way back when. It lived in North America, [pauses] a hundred fifty million years ago.
**WEX** [hesitant] : Fooled them how?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : For years, people thought it was a different dinosaur, [serious] called Brontosaurus. [amazed] Turns out, it's a very close cousin — [warmly] not quite the same animal.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Seventy-two feet long — [amazed] as long as two school buses. Standing up, fifteen feet tall — [curious] as tall as a double-decker bus. And twenty-three thousand kilos — [proud] as heavy as 8 hippos.
**WEX** [gasps] : 8 hippos.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yep. [whispers] And its neck alone was twenty feet long, for reaching leaves way up high.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, in a herd. [calm] It ate plants all day long — a huge amount of plants.
**WEX** [curious] : How long could it eat for?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Five hundred fifty pounds of plants EVERY day. [proud] As much as you eat in a whole year, in a single day.
**WEX** [gasps] : And did it have enemies, with a neck like that?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Allosaurus tried, mostly on the young ones. [serious] A full-grown adult wasn't worth the trouble — way too heavy to bring down.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : For a long time, this dinosaur was called [curious] "Brontosaurus" in every book.
**WEX** [curious] : And that wasn't its real name?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Scientists mixed up the label. [amazed] Today, we know: [confident] Apatosaurus and Brontosaurus are two very close cousins, but not the same animal.
