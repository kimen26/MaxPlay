## Archaeopteryx — Archaeopteryx lithographica

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : ar… kee… OP… ter… iks. In Greek, "archaeo" means ancient, very old. [serious] And "pteryx" is wing. [curious] So what does that give us?
**WEX** [confident] : The ancient wing.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : There you go. [proud] It lived in Europe, in Germany, one hundred fifty million years ago — it's one of the very first birds we know of.
**WEX** [gasps] : A bird that lived with the dinosaurs?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Exactly. [amazed] And it wasn't quite a bird like the ones we have today.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 0.5 meters long — [quickly] like a big cat stretched out! Standing up, it was only 0.3 meters tall — it would come up to your knees! And it weighed barely 1 kilo — [gently] light as a small bird.
**WEX** [curious] : That small, could it really fly?
**NARRATEUR H** [hesitant] : Scientists still debate it. [gently] It had real flight feathers, but maybe it flew poorly, or only over short distances.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a carnivore. [confident] It hunted small insects and small lizards, alone.
**WEX** [curious] : Did a bird have teeth?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Yes. [pauses] That's the strangest part: feathers like a bird, but teeth and claws like a dinosaur.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its fossil is one of the most precious in the whole world. [slowly] You can see feathers drawn in perfect detail, a long bony tail, [pauses] and sharp teeth inside its beak.
**WEX** [amazed] : Half dinosaur, half bird.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Exactly. [proud] It's kind of the link between the two worlds — one of the very first birds, with a whole lot of dinosaur still in it.
