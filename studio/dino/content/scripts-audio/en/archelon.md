## Archelon — Archelon ischyros

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [curious] : AR… keh… lon. "Arche" means chief, first — [serious] like in "archaeology." And "chelon" is turtle.
**WEX** [confident] : The chief turtle?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Right — the biggest turtle of all time. She lived seventy-five million years ago, [amazed] in a sea that cut [serious] North America right in half.
**WEX** [curious] : A turtle, but not a dinosaur?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : No, a real giant sea turtle — [warmly] a cousin of today's turtles, just a whole lot bigger.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : She was 4 meters long — like a small car! [amazed] She was as tall as a 4-year-old! And she weighed 2,200 kilos — [serious] as heavy as a rhino!
**WEX** [gasps] : A turtle as heavy as a rhino?
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : A turtle as heavy as a rhino, yes — [confident] but one that floated, thanks to her big flippers.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : She ate jellyfish, squid, soft-shelled creatures. [confident] And in her sea, two hunters could go after her: [nervous] Tylosaurus, a big mosasaur, and old sharks.
**WEX** [nervous] : Could she hide in her shell, like turtles do today?
**NARRATEUR H** [gently] : Not really — and that's her secret.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : Her shell wasn't hard like a backyard turtle's. [amazed] It was thick, tough skin, like leather — [softly] soft, almost like a wing.
**WEX** [curious] : A soft shell, what's that even good for?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : For swimming faster. With that shell, she flew through the water [confident] just like a leatherback turtle does today — [proud] the speed champion of all turtles.
