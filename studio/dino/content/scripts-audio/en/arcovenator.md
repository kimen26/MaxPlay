## Arcovenator — Arcovenator escotae

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : ar… koh… vuh… NAY… tor. [curious] The second piece, "venator," is Latin. It means the hunter.
**WEX** [curious] : And "arc"? Like a bow for shooting arrows?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Close! [proud] But it's the Arc, a river in the south of France, right near where its bones were found.
**WEX** [confident] : The hunter of the Arc.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : That's the whole name. [amazed] It lived in southern France, seventy-four million years ago.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Let's see how big. About 16 feet long — as long as a big SUV! [quickly] About 5 feet tall — as tall as a car — you'd have to look up! And about 900 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as 2 lions!
**WEX** [curious] : Why do you say "about"?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Because only a piece of its head was found, [curious] plus a few leg and tail bones. [confident] The rest, we guess from its cousin, Carnotaurus.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a hunter, a meat-eater. [confident] It probably hunted the plant-eaters of its island, [curious] like Rhabdodon.
**WEX** [curious] : But if almost everything is missing, how do we know it ate meat?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : From its teeth! [confident] They had jagged edges, like a bread knife. [softly] And the bones of three Arcovenator were found in the same place.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Here's how it was found: [curious] while a highway was being built! Scientists dug through the dirt of the building site, in Provence.
**WEX** [chuckles] : It was sleeping under the highway.
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Right next to it, yes. [amazed] And back then, France didn't look like today: [softly] it was a group of warm islands in the middle of the sea.
