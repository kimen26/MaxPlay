## Baryonyx — Baryonyx walkeri

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Bar… ee… on… iks. [curious] "Bary," in Greek, means heavy. [playful] And "onyx" means claw. The heavy claw!
**WEX** [amazed] : Onyx, like the shiny black stone? [confident] So that makes: the heavy claw.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly right, same word. [confident] A HUGE claw, 12 inches long, on its thumb. It lived in Europe — England, Spain, and Portugal, [amazed] one hundred twenty-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : What did it catch with a claw like that?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Fish. [amazed] Like a fishing bear, but a dinosaur.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 9 meters long — as long as a truck! [quickly] Standing up, it was 2.5 meters tall — as tall as a soccer goal! And it weighed 1,700 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as a small car and a cow put together! [serious] And it could swim about 14 miles an hour — as fast as Dad running.
**WEX** [gasps] : Did it have a long snout, like a crocodile?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes. [serious] A narrow snout full of pointy teeth, perfect for grabbing slippery fish.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a piscivore. [calm] It ate fish, [amazed] and scientists even found fish scales AND Iguanodon bones inside its stomach.
**WEX** [curious] : So it ate everything?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : A real foodie. [chuckles] Fish for the main course, and sometimes a little Iguanodon for dessert.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Baryonyx was discovered by a plumber, in England. [curious] He found enormous bones in a sand quarry, [amazed] and nobody knew what they were.
**WEX** [gasps] : A plumber finding a dinosaur?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Yes. [amazed] William Walker, the plumber who became famous. He's the one who found the heavy claw.
