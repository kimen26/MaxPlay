## Caletodraco — Caletodraco cottardi

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : kal… eh… toh… DRAY… koh. [curious] In Latin, "draco" means dragon.
**WEX** [playful] : A dragon that breathes fire?
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : No, it was a real dinosaur! [confident] And the first part, "Caletes," is a people from ancient Gaul who lived in Normandy.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it live with them?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Oh, no! The Caletes lived there two thousand years ago. [warmly] This one is a hundred million years old. [proud] The dragon of the Caletes, that's its whole name.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Let's see how big. About 20 feet long — as long as a road is wide — it takes up the whole street! [quickly] About 6 feet tall — as tall as a door! And about 1,500 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as a cow!
**WEX** [gasps] : It blocked the whole street?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Almost, yes. But those are guesses: [confident] only the bones of its hips and one tail bone were found. [curious] The rest, we guess from its cousin, Carnotaurus.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It belonged to a family of hunters, [confident] so it was probably a meat-eater. [curious] What it hunted? Nobody knows: no animal was found with it.
**WEX** [curious] : And how do we know its family, with so few bones?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Its hip bones look like those of Carnotaurus and its cousins. [amazed] They all lived in South America. [proud] This one is the first of the family found anywhere else.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its bones were at the foot of the cliffs in Normandy, in chalk, [curious] that white rock. [mischievously] But back then, that spot was under the sea!
**WEX** [curious] : A dinosaur that lived in the water?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : No, it lived on land, farther away. [confident] Its body probably floated out to sea after it died, [softly] before sinking to the bottom.
