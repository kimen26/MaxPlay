## Carcharodontosaurus — Carcharodontosaurus saharicus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Kar… kar… oh… don… toh… saurus. [curious] "Charcharo," in Greek, means jagged and sharp — it's the same root that gives us the word "shark." [serious] "Odonto" means tooth. And "saurus" means lizard.
**WEX** [playful] : Shark… tooth… I got "shark." [hesitant] So what's it all mean stuck together?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : The shark-toothed lizard. It lived in North Africa, [amazed] ninety-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it live with Spinosaurus?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Yes, same Africa. [confident] One hunted on land, [calm] the other in the water — they stayed out of each other's way.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 13 meters long — as long as a school bus! [quickly] Standing up, it was 3.8 meters tall — as tall as two Dads standing on each other's shoulders! And it weighed 7,000 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as an elephant and a rhino put together!
**WEX** [amazed] : Would it tower way over me?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : By a lot. [amazed] It's one of the biggest carnivores in the whole history of dinosaurs.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a carnivore. It hunted the big herbivores of Africa, all alone. [confident] In its territory, it was the most feared hunter of all.
**WEX** [curious] : So it ran the whole savanna?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : It ran the African savanna. [serious] Its teeth cut like a butcher's knife.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its teeth were 8 inches long [amazed] and serrated like a saw.
**WEX** [gasps] : A saw, right in its mouth?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : A living saw. The shark of dry land.
