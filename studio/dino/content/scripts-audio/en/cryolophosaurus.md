## Cryolophosaurus — Cryolophosaurus ellioti

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Kry… oh… loh… foh… saurus. [curious] "Cryo," in Greek, means ice, cold. [playful] "Lopho" means a crest. And "saurus" means lizard.
**WEX** [confident] : The frozen lizard, turned into an ice cube.
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : Not quite — the ice is just in its name. [amazed] The ice-crested lizard. It lived in Antarctica, one hundred ninety million years ago. [confident] But back then, Antarctica was covered in forests, warm and green.
**WEX** [amazed] : No ice at all?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : None at all. [amazed] Trees, plants, and plenty of plant-eaters to hunt, way down at the bottom of the world.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 6.5 meters long — as long as a road is wide, it takes up the whole street! [quickly] Standing up, it was 2.5 meters tall — as tall as a soccer goal! And it weighed 400 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as 2 lions!
**WEX** [amazed] : One carnivore, all alone, that far away?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes. [proud] The king of the bottom of the world, with no known rival its size.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a carnivore. [confident] It hunted the plant-eaters of Antarctica, in a great green forest.
**WEX** [curious] : A forest way down south, with plant-eaters?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Yep. [amazed] The forest fed plenty of plants, and so plenty of plant-eaters. [confident] Cryolophosaurus hunted them right there in all that green.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its crest stood up sideways across its head, [amazed] like a frozen wave [pauses] right above its eyes.
**WEX** [curious] : A crest going sideways?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Yes, the only known dinosaur with a crest shaped like that, [amazed] right between the eyes. A funny stone hairstyle.
