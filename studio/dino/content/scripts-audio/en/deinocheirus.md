## Deinocheirus — Deinocheirus mirificus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : DYE… no… KYE… rus. [curious] Two Greek words: "deino," terrible, [whispers] and "cheir," hand.
**WEX** [curious] : The terrible hand?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [proud] And its second name, "mirificus," means extraordinary. [amazed] It lived in Mongolia, in what is now the Gobi Desert, 70 million years ago.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : 36 feet long — as long as a school bus! [quickly] At the hip, about 11 feet tall — like two Dads standing on each other's shoulders! And about 14,000 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as an elephant and a rhino put together!
**WEX** [gasps] : And how big were its arms?
**NARRATEUR H** [proud] : 8 feet long: [amazed] the biggest arms of any two-legged dinosaur, with big claws at the end.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [calm] : It walked slowly along the rivers, [curious] grazing on plants with a wide, flat beak, like a duck's. [confident] It ate fish too.
**WEX** [curious] : And what were its big claws for?
**NARRATEUR H** [hesitant] : Scientists still aren't sure: [curious] digging, pulling down branches, or defending itself. [serious] Because Tarbosaurus, T. rex's big cousin, lived in the same place.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : In 1965, in the Gobi Desert, someone found two giant arms. [whispers] Just the arms! [curious] For almost 50 years, nobody knew what the rest looked like.
**WEX** [amazed] : A dinosaur we only knew by its arms.
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Then in 2014, surprise: [excited] tall spines on its back, a duck beak, [playful] and more than a thousand little stones in its belly to crush its food.
