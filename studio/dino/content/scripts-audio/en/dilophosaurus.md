**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Dye… loh… foh… saurus. [curious] "Di" means two in Greek. "Lopho" means a crest. [playful] And "saurus" means the…

**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Got it! Two crests standing up on its head. [confident] It lived in North America, [amazed] one hundred ninety-three million years ago — one of the very first big hunters in history.
**WEX** [amazed] : Crests like a crown?
