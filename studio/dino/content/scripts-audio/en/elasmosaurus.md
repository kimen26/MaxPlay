## Elasmosaurus — Elasmosaurus platyurus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [curious] : ee… LAZ… moh… SOR… us. In Greek, "elasmo" means a thin metal plate. [serious] His shoulder bones were flat, like little plates, [confident] so that's what scientists named him.
**WEX** [playful] : The plate lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Close. The thin-plated lizard. He lived eighty million years ago, [amazed] in a huge sea that cut [serious] North America right in half.
**WEX** [gasps] : A sea in the middle of the land?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Exactly. That whole region was underwater, back then.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 13 meters long — as long as a school bus! [amazed] He was as tall as a 4-year-old! And he weighed 2,500 kilos — [serious] as heavy as 5 horses!
**WEX** [curious] : Thirteen meters, but he was pretty skinny, right?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Very skinny, yes — mostly because of his neck. [amazed] The neck alone is already half his whole length.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He hunted fish and squid, in small groups. [confident] But in that sea, one hunter was even stronger than he was.
**WEX** [nervous] : Which one?
**NARRATEUR H** [calm] : Mosasaurus. In that sea, he was the boss — [gently] even Elasmosaurus had to watch out.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : In 1868, a scientist put his skeleton together for the very first time. [amazed] And he got it wrong — he glued the head onto the TAIL, [playful] thinking it was the neck.
**WEX** [gasps] : He put the head on backward?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Backward, yes. Another scientist pointed out the mistake — [chuckles] the biggest blunder in the history of dinosaurs.
