## Euoplocephalus — Euoplocephalus tutus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : YOO… oh… ploh… SEF… uh… lus. In Greek, "eu" means well. [curious] And "oplo" is a weapon, an armor — like the Greek soldiers called hoplites!
**WEX** [curious] : The well-armored head?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Right, "cephalo" means the head. [amazed] All together: the well-protected head. [calm] It lived in Alberta, Canada, seventy-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it look like Ankylosaurus?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : A cousin, but even more armored. [amazed] Even its eyelids were covered in bone.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Twenty feet long — [amazed] as long as a road is wide — it takes up the whole street. Standing up, five feet tall — [curious] as tall as a car — you'd have to look up. And two thousand two hundred kilos — [proud] as heavy as a rhino. [calm] And slow: about 4 miles an hour, like Dad walking fast.
**WEX** [gasps] : Its eyelids too, had ARMOR?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yep, [pauses] even its eyes were protected. [proud] The most armored dinosaur in all of history.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, mostly a loner. [calm] It grazed on plants calmly, never in a rush.
**WEX** [curious] : And who tried to catch it?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Gorgosaurus, a cousin of T. rex. [serious] But with armor from head to toe, that was far from a sure win.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its tail ended in a powerful club, [pauses] almost as strong as Ankylosaurus's.
**WEX** [amazed] : So Gorgosaurus rarely won.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Rarely, yes. [proud] An armored back, armored eyelids, a club at the end of its tail — barely anything to grab onto.
