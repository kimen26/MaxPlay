## Gallimimus — Gallimimus bullatus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : GAL… ih… MY… mus. "Galli," in Latin, [serious] means rooster, chicken. And "mimus" means one who imitates. [curious] So what does that give us?
**WEX** [confident] : The chicken imitator.
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : There you go, except it was much bigger than a chicken. [proud] It lived in Asia, in the Mongolian desert, seventy million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Is it a cousin of the raptors, [gasps] like Velociraptor?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : A distant cousin, yes, [amazed] but this one didn't even have teeth — just a beak, like a bird.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 6 meters long — [quickly] as long as a road is wide — it takes up the whole street! Standing up, it was 2 meters tall — as tall as a front door! And it weighed 440 kilos — [proud] as heavy as 2 lions. [amazed] And scientists think it could run up to 31 miles an hour — as fast as a car in the city.
**WEX** [gasps] : As fast as a car?
**NARRATEUR H** [proud] : Yes, with those long, thin legs. [amazed] One of the fastest dinosaurs we know of.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It ate a bit of everything — [confident] small animals, eggs, plants. It lived in big herds, like ostriches today.
**WEX** [curious] : And could T. rex catch it?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Not easily. [warmly] With its speed, Gallimimus would be gone before T. rex got close.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its speed came from long, thin legs and powerful muscles, [pauses] almost like an ostrich's.
**WEX** [amazed] : So it's really a giant ostrich.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Pretty much. [proud] A giant ostrich, without visible feathers, racing across the desert in a herd.
