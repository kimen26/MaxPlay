## Ichthyosaurus — Ichthyosaurus communis

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [curious] : IK… thee… oh… SOR… us. "Ichthys," in Greek, means fish. [serious] And "saurus," lizard.
**WEX** [confident] : The fish lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : That's the one. He lived one hundred ninety million years ago, [amazed] in the shallow seas of Europe.
**WEX** [curious] : A lizard that looks like a fish, does that really happen?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : It did, yes — but he wasn't a fish at all. [serious] He was a real reptile, shaped that way just to swim well.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 2 meters long — as long as a motorcycle! [amazed] Standing up, it would come up to your waist! And he weighed 150 kilos — [serious] as heavy as a donkey!
**WEX** [playful] : As heavy as a donkey, but with flippers?
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : With flippers, yes — [confident] made of fingers, like one big flat hand.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He hunted fish and squid, in groups, a bit like dolphins do today. [confident] But in his sea, something out there was even stronger than he was.
**WEX** [scared] : What?
**NARRATEUR H** [gently] : The big pliosaurs of his time, like Rhomaleosaurus. [warmly] Around them, Ichthyosaurus had to stay careful.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : He never laid eggs on land. [amazed] He gave birth to live babies, right in the water — [serious] just like whales and dolphins do today.
**WEX** [gasps] : So he never left the water, ever?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Never. His whole life, [warmly] from his very first day to his last, in the sea.
