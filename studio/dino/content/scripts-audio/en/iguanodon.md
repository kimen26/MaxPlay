## Iguanodon — Iguanodon bernissartensis

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : ig… WAH… noh… don. [curious] "Iguano" comes from iguana, a big lizard. And "odon," in Greek, is tooth.
**WEX** [confident] : The iguana tooth.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly, [warmly] his teeth really looked like an iguana's. He lived in Europe and North America, a hundred twenty-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Is he one of the very first dinosaurs ever found?
**NARRATEUR H** [proud] : Yes, one of the very first. [amazed] Discovered in Belgium, a very long time ago.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 10 meters long — as long as a truck! 2 meters 70 tall — [quickly] as tall as a soccer goal! And 3,000 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as a hippo!
**WEX** [gasps] : As tall as a soccer goal?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes, at shoulder height. [warmly] With a long tail, and a big duck bill.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He was a herbivore. He grazed in herds, [confident] walked on 4 legs, but could rear up on 2 to run fast when he got scared.
**WEX** [playful] : Like a kangaroo.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. Four legs to stay calm, [pauses] two legs for the sprint. Baryonyx watched him from a distance.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : On his thumb, he had a sharp bony spike. [whispers] At first, scientists placed it on his nose, like a horn.
**WEX** [gasps] : A horn that wasn't really a horn?
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : That's right. Actually, [confident] it was a secret weapon, tucked away on his thumb, for defense.
