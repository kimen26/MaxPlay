## Invictibellatrix — Invictibellatrix amberina

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : in… vik… tee… bel… LAY… triks. [curious] Two Latin words. "Bellatrix" means woman warrior.
**WEX** [curious] : And "invicti"?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Unbeaten. The one who never lost.
**WEX** [confident] : The warrior who was never beaten.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [proud] And "amberina" means "of amber," that hard, golden tree sap. [amazed] It lived in England, on the Isle of Wight, a hundred and twenty million years ago.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : About 11 feet long — like a small car! [quickly] About 5 feet tall — as tall as a car — you'd have to look up! And about 550 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as a tiger!
**WEX** [curious] : Why "about"? Can't we measure it?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Because only one piece of its skull was found. [confident] The rest, we guess from its cousins, the megaraptors, [curious] a family of two-legged hunters.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It's the first megaraptor known in Europe, [proud] and the smallest of them all. [confident] It maybe hunted small animals. [nervous] A bigger hunter lived in the same place, Neovenator: it was probably best to stay away.
**WEX** [curious] : And what did its hands look like?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : That, we don't know: we don't have its hands. [confident] But its cousins had very long claws on their hands, [softly] so maybe it did too.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : That piece of skull sat in a drawer at the Natural History Museum in London [curious] since 1924. Nobody knew it was a brand-new kind of dinosaur. [amazed] Only in 2026 did scientists finally figure it out.
**WEX** [gasps] : More than a hundred years in a drawer?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Yes, more than a hundred years. [warmly] Sometimes a big discovery waits patiently in a drawer.
