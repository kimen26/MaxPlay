## Kentrosaurus — Kentrosaurus aethiopicus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : KEN… troh… SOR… us. [curious] In Greek, "kentron" means a spike, a sharp point.
**WEX** [curious] : The spiked lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Yep — it had them all over its back. [calm] It lived in Africa, in Tanzania, a hundred fifty-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it know Brachiosaurus, then?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Its African cousin, Giraffatitan, [proud] lived in that exact same spot, at the exact same time.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Fifteen feet long — [amazed] as long as a big SUV. Standing up, six feet tall — [curious] as tall as Dad standing up. And one thousand one hundred kilos — [proud] as heavy as a small car. [calm] It walked calmly: 4 miles an hour, [pauses] like Dad walking fast.
**WEX** [gasps] : As tall as Dad.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yep — [playful] but way more spiky than him.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, maybe in a herd. [amazed] Spikes down the middle of its back, on its hips, [pauses] and up to two feet long on its tail.
**WEX** [curious] : And did carnivores dare get close?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : From the front, maybe. [serious] But never from behind: its spiked tail hit way too hard.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Kentrosaurus had even more spikes than its cousin Stegosaurus — [pauses] all the way to its hips.
**WEX** [amazed] : The spikiest one in the family.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Exactly. [proud] A small dinosaur, but a whole forest of spikes you'd never want to approach from behind.
