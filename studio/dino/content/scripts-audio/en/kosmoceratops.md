## Kosmoceratops — Kosmoceratops richardsoni

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Kozz… moh… SERR… uh… tops. [curious] Three Greek words: "kosmos," decorated, like a fancy costume. "Cerat" is horn. [whispers] And "ops" is face.
**WEX** [curious] : So it's the decorated horned face?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [warmly] He lived in Utah, in North America, seventy-six million years ago. [curious] His second name, "richardsoni," honors Scott Richardson, a volunteer who found his skull in the desert.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : 15 feet long — as long as a big SUV! [quickly] About 5 feet tall — as tall as a car — you'd have to look up! And about 2,600 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as a cow and a horse put together!
**WEX** [curious] : And his frill, was it big?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Pretty short, for a horned dinosaur. [proud] But lined all around with spikes, like a crown.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [calm] : He was a herbivore. [warmly] He munched on ferns, cycads and conifers, near rivers and big swamps. [curious] Other horned dinosaurs lived with him, like Utahceratops.
**WEX** [nervous] : Was there a hunter out there?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Yes, TEH-rah-toh-FOH-nee-us, a tyrannosaur. [confident] The horns above his eyes could help him defend himself.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : Shall we count his horns? [softly] One on the nose... [serious] two above the eyes... [nervous] two on the cheeks... [excited] and ten HOOKS all around his frill!
**WEX** [gasps] : That makes fifteen.
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Fifteen horns and spikes: [proud] the most decorated head of any dinosaur we know. [curious] Scientists think they were mostly for showing off.
