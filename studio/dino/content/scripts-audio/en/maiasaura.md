## Maiasaura — Maiasaura peeblesorum

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : MY… ah… SOR… ah. [curious] In Greek, "maia" means good mother, nursemaid. And "saura" is lizard, in the feminine.
**WEX** [confident] : The good mother lizard.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [warmly] She lived in the United States, in Montana, seventy-six million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Why do they call her that, "good mother"?
**NARRATEUR H** [gently] : Because scientists found her nests, full of eggs and babies. [serious] Daspletosaurus hunted the young ones; [nervous] the little Troodon would sometimes come steal eggs.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : She was 9 meters long — as long as a truck! 2 meters 50 tall — [quickly] as tall as a soccer goal! And 2,500 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as 5 horses!
**WEX** [gasps] : As tall as a soccer goal, plus babies?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes. A big herbivore mom, [warmly] who kept her babies close by.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : She was a herbivore. She lived in a huge colony: [amazed] hundreds of nests, side by side.
**WEX** [curious] : So she took care of her babies?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Yes, [proud] and it was actually the very first proof that a dinosaur ever did that. [gently] She brought them food while they were too small to leave the nest.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : The nests were spaced about 23 feet [curious] apart from each other — [pauses] just the length of a mom, so she could turn around without crushing her neighbor's nest.
**WEX** [gasps] : So they'd measured it.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Exactly the right distance. [proud] A whole colony of moms, all well organized.
