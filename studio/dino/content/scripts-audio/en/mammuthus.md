## Woolly Mammoth — Mammuthus primigenius

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Mam… muth… us. [curious] That's a very old word that means something like "horn of the earth" — the first hunters who found its tusks frozen in the ground [amazed] thought they were horns.
**WEX** [curious] : A cousin of the elephant?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : A totally furry elephant, yes. [serious] And careful: it didn't live with the dinosaurs. It showed up way later, in the Ice Age, [pauses] a hundred thousand years ago, when the Earth was all cold.
**WEX** [gasps] : After the dinosaurs?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Long after. In Europe, Asia, and [warmly] North America, everywhere it was freezing.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 18 feet long — as long as a big SUV! Standing up, it was 11 feet tall — [amazed] as tall as two Dads standing on each other's shoulders! And it weighed 5,000 kilos — [proud] as heavy as an elephant!
**WEX** [curious] : What were its tusks for?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Sweeping away snow. [amazed] Its two curved tusks could be over 13 feet long — longer than Dad lying flat on the ground.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a herbivore. It ate grass and small plants hidden under the snow. [warmly] A thick coat of fur and a big layer of fat kept it warm, and it lived in herds, [gently] just like elephants today.
**WEX** [nervous] : Who could attack it, with all that fur?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Only prehistoric humans. [warmly] Nobody else dared go after an animal that big.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : The very last mammoths on the whole Earth survived on [pauses] a small island, way up north, long after all the others were gone.
**WEX** [gasps] : Long how?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Until just four thousand years ago. [slowly] Back then, the great pyramids of Egypt were already built.
