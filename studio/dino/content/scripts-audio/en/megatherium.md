## Megatherium — Megatherium americanum

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Meh… gah… THEER… ee… um. In Greek, "mega" means giant, huge. [proud] And "therium" means beast. The giant beast.
**WEX** [curious] : A giant dinosaur?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Nope, not a dinosaur — [serious] it showed up way later, in the Ice Age, [pauses] a million years ago. It was a giant sloth, in South America.
**WEX** [gasps] : A sloth?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : The biggest sloth that ever lived — [amazed] as big as an elephant, not like the one sleeping in a tree.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 20 feet long — as long as a road is wide, it blocked the whole street! Reared up on its back legs, it stood up to 11 feet tall — [amazed] as tall as two Dads standing on each other's shoulders! And it weighed 4,000 kilos — [proud] as heavy as 2 rhinos!
**WEX** [curious] : Why did it rear up?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : To reach the leaves way up in the trees, [warmly] where nobody else could eat.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a herbivore. It ate leaves and branches, [gently] mostly on its own, nice and calm.
**WEX** [curious] : Nobody bothered it?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Almost nobody. Its huge size protected it all by itself — [hesitant] except maybe its babies, which a Smilodon might have gone after.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : To eat, it stood straight up on its back legs, leaning on its big tail, [amazed] like sitting on a three-legged stool.
**WEX** [gasps] : A three-legged stool?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : Exactly. And its front claws were as long as your forearm, [slowly] for pulling branches toward it.
