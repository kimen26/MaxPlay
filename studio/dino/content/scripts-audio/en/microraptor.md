## Microraptor — Microraptor gui

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : MY… kroh… RAP… tor. "Micro," in Greek, [serious] means tiny — like a microbe. And "raptor" is thief. [curious] So?
**WEX** [confident] : The little thief.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [proud] It lived in China, in Asia, one hundred twenty million years ago.
**WEX** [gasps] : Is it the smallest raptor you know about?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : One of the smallest dinosaurs ever found, yes. [playful] And it has a secret nobody else has.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 0.77 meters long — [quickly] like a big cat stretched out! Standing up, it was only 0.3 meters tall — it would come up to your knees! And it weighed barely 1 kilo — [gently] light as a small bird.
**WEX** [curious] : That tiny, and still a carnivore?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Yes. [proud] Tiny but it ate anything — lizards, fish, small animals.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It lived in the trees of its homeland, [confident] to escape bigger carnivores and large reptiles.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it fly, like a bird?
**NARRATEUR H** [gently] : Not quite. [pauses] It glided, from tree to tree, a bit like a flying squirrel.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its secret is that it had [amazed] four wings: two on its arms, [pauses] and two more on its legs.
**WEX** [gasps] : Four wings, all at once?
**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : All four together, spread open, worked like a little glider. [softly] It would jump from a tree, [slowly] spread its four wings, and glide through the air to the next one.
