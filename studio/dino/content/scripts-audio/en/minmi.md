## Minmi — Minmi paravertebra

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : MIN… mee. [curious] This name isn't Greek or Latin like the others — it's the name of a place in Australia.
**WEX** [curious] : A place?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Minmi Crossing, [pauses] where its bones were found. [calm] It lived in Queensland, a hundred twenty million years ago.
**WEX** [amazed] : Was this dino small?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Tiny — and for a long time, [pauses] it even held the record for the shortest dinosaur name in the world.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Ten feet long — [amazed] like three 4-year-olds lying end to end. Standing up, three feet tall — [curious] as tall as a 4-year-old. And three hundred kilos — [proud] as heavy as 2 donkeys.
**WEX** [gasps] : 2 donkeys, for such a little dino?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yep, [pauses] all that weight came from its armor. [amazed] But it had longer legs than its armored cousins.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, mostly a loner. [calm] Armor on its back, and even under its belly — that's rare.
**WEX** [hesitant] : And what were its long legs for?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Dashing off! [pauses] If a hunter got close, it took off running before it even had to fight.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Scientists found its very last meal in its belly — seeds, [pauses] bits of fruit, chewed-up ferns.
**WEX** [amazed] : Its last meal, really?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Really. [proud] It's super rare to know EXACTLY what a dinosaur ate right before it died.
