## Mosasaurus — Mosasaurus hoffmannii

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : MOH… sah… SOR… us. His name comes from a river — [curious] the Meuse, in Europe, where his first bones were found. And "saurus," [confident] you're starting to know that one.
**WEX** [confident] : Lizard. The Meuse River lizard.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. Except he didn't live in a river — [amazed] he swam in every ocean on Earth, [serious] seventy million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Every ocean? Even Tritri's?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : That very one. Mosasaurus and Triceratops lived at the same time, [serious] right at the end of the age of dinosaurs — him in the water, [warmly] Triceratops on land.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 17 meters long — as long as a school bus and a half! [amazed] He was as wide-bodied as Dad's car! And he weighed 10,000 kilos — [serious] as heavy as 2 elephants!
**WEX** [gasps] : Two elephants, swimming?
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : Two swimming elephants, yes — and a whole lot faster than they are.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He was a lone hunter, and the boss of his whole ocean. [confident] He ate sharks, big fish, and other marine reptiles.
**WEX** [nervous] : And nobody hunted him?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Nobody. He was at the very top, [confident] in every ocean on Earth — no animal could touch him.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : Mosasaurus didn't stay under the water. [amazed] He'd leap right out of the waves to snatch flying reptiles, like Pteranodon, [excited] straight out of the sky.
**WEX** [gasps] : He caught things that fly?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : From the water, yes. One snap of his jaws, and — dinner served.
