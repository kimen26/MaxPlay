## Ophthalmosaurus — Ophthalmosaurus icenicus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [curious] : off… THAL… moh… SOR… us. "Ophthalmo" means eye — like an ophthalmologist, [serious] the doctor who checks your eyes. And "saurus," lizard.
**WEX** [confident] : The eye lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : That's the one. He lived one hundred fifty million years ago, [amazed] in the shallow seas of Europe and North America.
**WEX** [curious] : Why such big eyes, for a sea lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : That's exactly his thing. We're getting there.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 4 meters long — like a small car! [amazed] He was as tall as a 4-year-old! And he weighed as heavy as a small car — [serious] the size AND the weight of one, all in one animal!
**WEX** [playful] : A car that weighs a car?
**NARRATEUR H** [chuckles] : Exactly — the size [confident] and the weight of a small car, all wrapped up in one.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He hunted fish and squid, in groups, in warm waters. [confident] But in those seas, [nervous] big predators like Liopleurodon were prowling too.
**WEX** [nervous] : Did he have to be careful?
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Yes — but he had a weapon for that. His eyes.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : His eyes were 9 inches wide. [amazed] Almost his whole head, [serious] just for seeing in the pitch-dark depths.
**WEX** [gasps] : Nine inches, like a basketball?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Pretty close. And thanks to that, [confident] he spotted prey where nobody else could see a thing.
