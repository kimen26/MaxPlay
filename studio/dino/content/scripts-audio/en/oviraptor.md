## Oviraptor — Oviraptor philoceratops

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : OH… vih… RAP… tor. "Ovi," in Latin, [serious] means egg — like in "oval." And "raptor" is thief. [curious] So what does that give us?
**WEX** [confident] : The egg thief.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : That's its name. [proud] It lived in Asia, in the Mongolian desert, seventy-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Is it a cousin of the raptors, [gasps] like Velociraptor?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : A cousin, yes, [amazed] but this one had no teeth — a beak, a pretty colorful crest, almost like a parrot.
**WEX** [gasps] : An egg thief? [nervous] That's not very nice.
**NARRATEUR H** [gently] : Wait for the end of the story. [warmly] It doesn't deserve that name at all.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 2 meters long — [quickly] as long as a motorcycle! Standing up, it was 0.9 meters tall — as tall as a 4-year-old! And it weighed 35 kilos — [proud] as heavy as a big 10-year-old.
**WEX** [curious] : And its crest, what was that for?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Probably to look good, [playful] a bit like a peacock's tail.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It ate a bit of everything — [confident] small animals, plants, maybe eggs too. It lived in pairs to raise its young.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it have enemies?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Carnivores like Velociraptor. [serious] It had to stay alert, especially near its nest.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Listen to this. [serious] The very first Oviraptor fossil was found [pauses] right next to a nest of eggs. Scientists thought it had just stolen them.
**WEX** [gasps] : So it really was stealing eggs.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : No, actually. [hesitant] Much later, another Oviraptor was found, [whispers] sitting on its nest, arms spread wide, brooding.
**WEX** [amazed] : Its own eggs.
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Its own eggs. [proud] It was a good dad, not a thief. Its name, it turns out, is completely unfair.
