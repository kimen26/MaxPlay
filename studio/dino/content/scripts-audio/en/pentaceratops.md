## Pentaceratops — Pentaceratops sternbergii

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : PEN… tah… SEH… rah… tops. [curious] "Penta," in Greek, means five. "Cerat" is horn. And "ops" is face.
**WEX** [confident] : The five-horned face.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Almost: three real horns, [pauses] plus two bony points on his cheeks. [warmly] He lived in New Mexico, in North America, seventy-five million years ago.
**WEX** [nervous] : Did he have enemies out there?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : A big hunter named Bistahieversor.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 6 meters long — as long as a road is wide, he took up the whole street! 2 meters tall — [quickly] as tall as a front door! And 5,000 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as an elephant! Scientists think he could run about 16 miles an hour — as fast as a bike going fast.
**WEX** [gasps] : As heavy as an elephant.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes. A real heavyweight, [proud] with a head even more impressive than his body.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He was a herbivore. He grazed on plants in herds. [confident] His huge frill worked like a shield against hunters.
**WEX** [curious] : So Bistahieversor didn't dare attack him?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Let's say he [hesitant] thought twice about it. A giant bony shield tends to cool things down.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : His head, with the frill, measured OVER 3 meters. [whispers] One of the biggest heads of any animal that ever walked the Earth.
**WEX** [gasps] : Bigger than Dad standing up.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Much bigger. Pentaceratops, the champion of the giant head.
