## Plateosaurus — Plateosaurus engelhardti

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : PLAT… ee… oh… SOR… us. [curious] In Greek, "platus" means wide, flat.
**WEX** [curious] : The flat lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : That's its name, yes — [amazed] even though it wasn't flat at all. [calm] It lived in Europe, in Germany, France, and Switzerland, two hundred ten million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Two hundred ten million? Is that before T. rex?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Way before. [proud] Plateosaurus is one of the very first big plant-eating dinosaurs in all of history.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Twenty-six feet long — [amazed] like two cars, one behind the other. Standing up, nine feet tall — [curious] as tall as a basketball hoop. And two thousand kilos — [proud] as heavy as a rhino.
**WEX** [gasps] : A rhino, for one of the very first ones?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yep — [whispers] already a real giant, for its time.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, in a herd. [calm] It mostly walked on all four legs.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it have enemies, back in such an ancient time?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Big four-legged hunters prowled around Europe. [serious] But no big two-legged carnivore existed yet — [calm] those would come much later.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Plateosaurus could rear straight up on its back legs, [pauses] like a bear standing up.
**WEX** [amazed] : To reach the tallest branches.
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Exactly. [proud] A pioneer, on all fours or standing on two, depending on what it wanted to eat.
