## Protoceratops — Protoceratops andrewsi

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : PROH… toh… SEH… rah… tops. [curious] "Proto," in Greek, means the first. "Cerat" is horn. And "ops" is face.
**WEX** [confident] : The first horned face.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Almost: he didn't [curious] have a real horn yet, just a small bump on his nose. [warmly] The very first ancestor of the horned dinos. He lived in Mongolia, in Asia, seventy-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Who did he live with, out there?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Velociraptor hunted in that same desert.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 2 meters 20 long — as long as a motorcycle! 0 point 75 meters tall — [gently] it would come up to your waist! And 80 kilos — as heavy as Dad! [amazed] Scientists think he could run about 16 miles an hour — as fast as a bike going fast.
**WEX** [curious] : Small, but heavy as Dad?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Compact and tough. A really muscular little dinosaur.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He was a herbivore. He crushed tough plants with his powerful jaw, [confident] in herds, with nests grouped close together.
**WEX** [nervous] : And was he scared of Velociraptor?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : No. [warmly] He defended himself with his sharp beak and his frill. Small, but not easy to catch.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : In the Gobi Desert, scientists [amazed] found HUNDREDS of Protoceratops skeletons — eggs, babies, young ones, adults. [whispers] We know his whole life story, from tiny to full-grown.
**WEX** [gasps] : His whole life, in stone.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Yes. One of the best-known dinosaurs on Earth.
