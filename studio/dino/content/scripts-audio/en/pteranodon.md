## Pteranodon — Pteranodon longiceps

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : teh… RAN… oh… don. In Greek, "pter" means wing. "An" means without. [serious] And "odon" is tooth. [curious] So what does that give us?
**WEX** [confident] : The wing without teeth.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly, a big beak without a single tooth. [proud] It lived in North America, eighty-five million years ago.
**WEX** [gasps] : Is it a dinosaur too?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : No, actually. [amazed] It's not a dinosaur at all — it's a flying reptile, a pterosaur.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its open wings spanned 6 meters wingtip to wingtip — wide as a car and a half! Laid end to end, that's [quickly] as long as a road is wide — it takes up the whole street! Standing up, it was 1.8 meters tall — [proud] as tall as Dad standing up! And it weighed 25 kilos — as heavy as a dog. [amazed] And flying, scientists think it could go 20 miles an hour — as fast as a horse cantering.
**WEX** [curious] : And that funny crest, [gasps] behind its head?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : It may have helped steer its flight, [slowly] like a rudder.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It ate fish and squid. [confident] It glided for a long time without effort, then dove into the sea to fish.
**WEX** [curious] : And in the water, could anything catch it?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : A big marine reptile, Mosasaurus, [gently] hunted in the same waters. [serious] Once it landed on the water to fish, Pteranodon had to stay alert.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It lived in colonies on the cliffs, [pauses] like seagulls today, but giant.
**WEX** [amazed] : A cliff full of Pteranodons.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Hundreds, maybe, [proud] all ready to glide off together toward the sea to fish.
