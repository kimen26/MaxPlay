## Scelidosaurus — Scelidosaurus harrisonii

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : skeh… LY… doh… SOR… us. In Greek, "saurus" is lizard. [curious] And "scelido" was supposed to mean "strong legs," [amazed] because it had really powerful thighs.
**WEX** [curious] : A strong-legged lizard.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Yes. But here's the thing: [amazed] the scientist who named it got the Greek word wrong — the one he actually wrote means "rib of beef." [playful] Without meaning to, he named it the beef-rib lizard.
**WEX** [curious] : And where did the beef-rib lizard live?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : In England, by the sea, a hundred ninety-three million years ago. [curious] And already, [pauses] small two-legged hunters were prowling the area.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Thirteen feet long — [amazed] like a small car. Standing up, four feet tall — [curious] as tall as a 4-year-old. And two hundred seventy kilos — [proud] as heavy as a tiger. [calm] It moved without rushing: about 9 miles an hour, [pauses] like a bike riding chill.
**WEX** [curious] : As heavy as a tiger, for a plant-eater?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yep. [whispers] Most of that weight was its bony armor — and those strong legs carried it just fine.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore. It grazed on low plants, ferns, on all four legs. [amazed] Its skin was covered in tiny bony studs, [curious] lined up in rows from neck to tail — like buttons.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it live with friends?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : A mystery — nobody knows yet. [serious] But with that armor, even alone, it wasn't afraid of much.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Scelidosaurus is one of the very first dinosaurs ever found almost ENTIRELY whole. [amazed] An English worker discovered it [curious] in a cliff, almost a hundred seventy years ago.
**WEX** [gasps] : Almost whole, in a cliff.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Yep. And its skeleton is still on display today, in London's great museum. [proud] They named it "harrisonii," [warmly] as a gift to the man who found it.
