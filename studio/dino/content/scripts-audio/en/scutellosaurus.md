## Scutellosaurus — Scutellosaurus lawleri

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : skoo… TEL… oh… SOR… us. [curious] In Latin, "scutellum" means a little shield.
**WEX** [curious] : The little-shield lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Yep — because of the hundreds of tiny bony plates on its back. [calm] It lived in Arizona, a hundred ninety-six million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Were there dangers around, back then?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Dilophosaurus, a big crested hunter, [serious] prowled that same area.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Four feet long — [amazed] like a big dog — a Labrador. Standing up, [curious] it would come up to your knees. And four kilos — [proud] as heavy as a big cat.
**WEX** [gasps] : So tiny.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Tiny, yes. [amazed] But it already ran on its two back legs.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, alone or in a small group. [calm] It grazed on low plants.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it run fast, to escape Dilophosaurus?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : Its long tail helped it balance while running, [pauses] like a tightrope pole. The only armored dino that ran on two legs.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Over three hundred tiny bony plates covered its back and tail, [pauses] like a mosaic.
**WEX** [amazed] : But no big armor like its cousins.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Not yet. [proud] It was the very first version — light and early, before Ankylosaurus and its clubs.
