## Stegosaurus — Stegosaurus ungulatus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : STEG… oh… SOR… us. [curious] In Greek, "stego" means roof.
**WEX** [curious] : The roofed lizard?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Yep — the scientist who found it thought its big plates lay flat on its back, [amazed] like roof tiles. [calm] It lived in North America and Europe, a hundred fifty-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Were they lying flat, its plates?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Nope! [pauses] Scientists later discovered they stood up straight, running along the whole length of its back.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Thirty feet long — [amazed] as long as a truck. Standing up, nine feet tall — [curious] as tall as a basketball hoop. And four thousand kilos — [proud] as heavy as 2 rhinos. [calm] It walked calmly: 4 miles an hour, [pauses] like Dad walking fast.
**WEX** [gasps] : Not very fast, then.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Nope — [serious] it didn't need to be. Its tail did the work.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : Herbivore, maybe in a small group. [calm] Its big colorful plates probably worked like a radiator, [pauses] to warm up or cool down.
**WEX** [curious] : And for defense?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Its tail had 4 long spikes at the end. [serious] Allosaurus had to be very careful getting close.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its brain was tiny — [pauses] about the size of a walnut, for such a huge animal.
**WEX** [amazed] : Such a little head, on such a big body.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : True, but its spiked tail aimed true — [proud] it could pierce right through a careless Allosaurus.
