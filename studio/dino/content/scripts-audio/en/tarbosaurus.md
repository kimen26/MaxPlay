## Tarbosaurus — Tarbosaurus bataar

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Tar… boh… saurus. [curious] "Tarbo," in Greek, means fear, dread — the one who makes everyone tremble. And "saurus" means lizard.
**WEX** [proud] : The lizard who brings fear, I got that one right away.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Perfect. [confident] It was T. rex's cousin, in Asia, [amazed] in the Gobi Desert, seventy million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it really look like T. rex?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : A lot. [confident] Same family of hunters, just the Mongolia version.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 12 meters long — as long as a school bus! [quickly] Standing up, it was 4.5 meters tall — as tall as a double-decker bus! [serious] And it weighed 5,000 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as an elephant!
**WEX** [amazed] : A little lighter than T. rex?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : A little lighter. [proud] But plenty enough to rule the Gobi Desert.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a carnivore. It hunted the big herbivores of Mongolia, [confident] like Therizinosaurus. [calm] Mostly a loner.
**WEX** [curious] : How did Therizinosaurus defend itself?
**NARRATEUR H** [playful] : With its ENORMOUS claws, three feet long, [serious] lined up like a fence of knives. [confident] Faced with that, Tarbosaurus usually went looking for an easier meal.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It had 60 sharp teeth, [serious] and it shook its prey hard before swallowing it.
**WEX** [gasps] : Like a dog with a rope toy?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Exactly like that. [amazed] Except the rope toy was a whole dinosaur.
