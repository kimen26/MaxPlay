## Triceratops — Triceratops horridus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Try… SEH… rah… tops. [curious] In Greek, "tri" means three. "Cerat" is horn. And "ops" is face. [pauses] So, what do you think that spells out?
**WEX** [confident] : The three-horned face.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [warmly] He lived in North America, sixty-six million years ago.
**WEX** [gasps] : Wait. [softly] That's Tritri.
**NARRATEUR H** [warmly] : Yes. [happily] The real Triceratops. He lived alongside Torosaurus and Edmontosaurus — and T. rex was prowling nearby.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : He was 9 meters long — as long as a truck! [quickly] 3 meters tall — as tall as a basketball hoop! And 9,000 kilos — [amazed] as heavy as 3 hippos!
**WEX** [gasps] : Three hippos, in one dino.
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes. And built [proud] like a tank: his bones and horns were super thick.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : He was a herbivore. He grazed on low plants and ferns, [confident] maybe in herds, to better protect the young.
**WEX** [nervous] : And T. rex, did he attack him?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : He tried. [warmly] But Tritri fought back with his three horns. Triceratops never backed down from anyone.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : His horns were OVER a meter long. [whispers] And scientists found T. rex bones... with Triceratops horn marks on them.
**WEX** [gasps] : They really fought.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : For real. Real battle scars, carved right into the bone.
