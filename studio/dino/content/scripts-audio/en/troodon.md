## Troodon — Troodon formosus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : TROH… oh… don. In Greek, the first part means "to wound," [serious] and "odon" is tooth. [curious] So what does that give us?
**WEX** [confident] : The wounding tooth.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : There you go. [proud] It lived in North America, seventy-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : What was special about this one?
**NARRATEUR H** [proud] : One huge thing: [amazed] it might be the smartest dinosaur we know of.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 2.2 meters long — [quickly] as long as a motorcycle! Standing up, it was 0.9 meters tall — as tall as a 4-year-old! And it weighed 50 kilos — [proud] as heavy as a wolf. [amazed] And scientists think it could run 31 miles an hour — as fast as a car in the city.
**WEX** [gasps] : As fast as a car?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes, with long, thin legs, [proud] built for running.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It ate a bit of everything — [confident] small mammals, lizards, and sometimes plants. [pauses] Its eyes were huge, for seeing well at night.
**WEX** [curious] : Why did it have such a big brain?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Actually, [warmly] nobody's really sure. But for its size, its brain was the biggest of any dinosaur we know of.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Some scientists have even imagined a wild question: [serious] if the dinosaurs had never gone extinct, [pauses] would Troodon have become as smart as a human, millions of years later?
**WEX** [gasps] : Like us?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : It's just an idea, [hesitant] nobody can prove it. But just thinking about it is fascinating.
