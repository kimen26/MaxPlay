## Tsintaosaurus — Tsintaosaurus spinorhinus

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : TSIN… tow… SOR… us. [curious] "Tsintao" is the old name of a big city in China, Qingdao. [proud] And "saurus," in Greek, means lizard.
**WEX** [curious] : The lizard of Tsintao?
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [amazed] Its second name, "spy… no… RYE… nus," means "spine nose." [confident] It lived in China, 75 million years ago.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : 28 feet long — as long as a truck! [quickly] At the hip, 10 feet tall — as tall as a basketball hoop! And about 5,500 pounds — [amazed] as heavy as 5 horses!
**WEX** [curious] : Did it walk on four legs?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Most of the time, yes. [playful] But it could rear up on its back legs, to reach high leaves or keep watch.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [calm] : A plant-eater, with a duck beak. [curious] Behind its beak, hundreds of little teeth ground up plants, [proud] like a grater.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it live all alone?
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : Probably in a herd, like its duck-billed cousins. [serious] In the same region lived a big cousin of T. rex, Zhuchengtyrannus.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : For 55 years, people drew it with a thin horn on its head, [playful] like a unicorn!
**WEX** [gasps] : A unicorn dinosaur?
**NARRATEUR H** [amazed] : That's what everyone thought. [confident] But in 2013, scientists realized that horn was only the bottom of a big rounded crest. [softly] The top is still missing: we draw it from its cousins.
