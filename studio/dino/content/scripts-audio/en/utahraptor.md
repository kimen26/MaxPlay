## Utahraptor — Utahraptor ostrommaysorum

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : YOO… tah… RAP… tor. "Utah" is the American state where its bones were found. And "raptor," in Latin, [curious] is thief. So what does that give us?
**WEX** [confident] : The thief from Utah.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : There you go. [proud] It lived right there, in North America, one hundred twenty-six million years ago.
**WEX** [gasps] : Is it a cousin of Velociraptor?
**NARRATEUR H** [proud] : Yes, but a much bigger cousin. [amazed] It's the biggest raptor we know of.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 6 meters long — [quickly] as long as a road is wide — it takes up the whole street! Standing up, it was 2 meters tall — as tall as a front door! And it weighed 425 kilos — [proud] as heavy as 2 lions.
**WEX** [curious] : And its claw, [gasps] was that huge too?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : [amazed] The biggest of any raptor: about 9 inches. Almost as long as your whole hand and your arm to the elbow.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a carnivore. It hunted big plant-eaters, [confident] maybe in a hunting team rather than alone.
**WEX** [curious] : A team, like in soccer?
**NARRATEUR H** [hesitant] : Scientists aren't sure, [gently] but it's possible: several raptors together could take down prey much bigger than themselves.
**WEX** [scared] : And could anyone catch it?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : In its land, at its time, [warmly] nothing big enough has ever been found. It was the top hunter.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Its giant claw, [slowly] shaped like a sickle, was for slashing and hooking. [serious] One strike could open a deep wound in its prey.
**WEX** [amazed] : Like a real knife.
**NARRATEUR H** [softly] : A knife the size of your hand, yes. [proud] With that, even prey twice its size wasn't out of reach.
