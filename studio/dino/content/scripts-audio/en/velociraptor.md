## Velociraptor — Velociraptor mongoliensis

### BLOC A — Présentation

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Veh… LOS… ih… RAP… tor. In Latin, "veloci" means fast — like "velocity." [curious] And "raptor" is a thief. Can you guess?
**WEX** [confident] : The fast thief.
**NARRATEUR H** [happily] : Exactly. [proud] It lived in Asia, in the Mongolian desert, seventy-five million years ago.
**WEX** [curious] : Did it hunt in a big pack, [nervous] like in the movies?
**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : No, that's [gently] a myth. The real Velociraptor mostly hunted alone, or in pairs.

### BLOC B — Taille

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : It was 2 meters long — [quickly] as long as a motorcycle! Standing up, it was only half a meter tall — [playful] it would come up to your waist! And it weighed 15 kilos — as heavy as a 4-year-old.
**WEX** [gasps] : That small?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : Yes. [amazed] In the movies they show it giant, with no feathers, but the real Velociraptor was covered in feathers, [proud] barely bigger than a big dog.

### BLOC C — Comment il vivait

**NARRATEUR H** [serious] : It was a carnivore. Its big weapon was a sickle-shaped claw, [confident] on each back foot, for hooking prey and never letting go. [slowly] It hunted small dinosaurs and lizards.
**WEX** [curious] : And who could catch it?
**NARRATEUR H** [confident] : The biggest carnivores in its desert. [warmly] But with its feathers and its speed, it wasn't easy prey.

### BLOC D — Le truc fou

**NARRATEUR H** [excited] : Listen to this true story. [serious] In the Mongolian desert, scientists found an incredible fossil: a Velociraptor and a Protoceratops, [pauses] frozen mid-fight.
**WEX** [gasps] : Mid-fight?
**NARRATEUR H** [whispers] : Yes. The raptor's claw stuck in the other one's neck, [slowly] and the other one biting down on its arm. [softly] A sand dune collapsed on top of them, and they stayed like that, together, for seventy-five million years.
